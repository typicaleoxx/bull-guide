title: Computer Science Course Registration Advice
source: Locally created sample document based on common university registration experiences
topic: Course Registration

Students recommend checking prerequisites and degree requirements before registration opens. A course may appear available in the schedule but still require a specific prerequisite, laboratory section, or department approval.

It is useful to prepare more than one possible schedule. Preferred sections may fill quickly, and required courses may overlap. Students often create a primary schedule and one or two backup combinations before their registration time.

Students should consider workload in addition to credit hours. Three programming intensive courses may require more time than a schedule containing a mixture of technical and general education courses.

Waitlists can change during the first week of the semester, but students should not assume that a seat will become available. They recommend registering for a reasonable backup course while monitoring the preferred section.

When degree progress is unclear, students recommend reviewing the degree audit and contacting an academic advisor. Advice from other students can explain workload and course order, but official prerequisite and graduation decisions should be verified with the university.
